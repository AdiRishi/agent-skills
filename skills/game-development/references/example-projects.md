# Example projects

Choose an example that matches the current problem. Inspect the relevant visuals, build steps, or source, and adapt what helps the project's own art direction and requirements.

## Evergrow: procedural 2D art and visual review

[Public source](https://github.com/Dimillian/Evergrow) · [Concept gallery](https://github.com/Dimillian/Evergrow/tree/main/docs/concepts)

Consult for translating generated concept art into code-drawn environments, characters, and equipment. Explore the shared character poses and equipment geometry, and the isolated review scenes used to examine visuals without playing through the whole game. The concept gallery preserves images and prompts for comparison with the implementation.

## Void Explorer: coherent worlds and authored spacecraft

[Showcase and playable game](https://developers.openai.com/showcase/void-explorer)

Consult for carrying a visual direction from orbital flight through atmospheric descent and landing. The walkthrough connects generated gameplay concepts, a playable slice, rendering improvements, and a Blender spacecraft built from consistent reference views. Inspect how the ship and procedural world maintain a shared style across changes in scale.

## Sunwake: stylized water and an authored boat

[Showcase and playable game](https://developers.openai.com/showcase/sunwake)

Consult for faceted ocean art, lighting, and integrating a Blender vehicle into a procedural environment. Study the progression from water concepts to sailing, buoyancy, wakes, spray, and refinement of the boat's contact with the sea.

## Hollowflux: reactive 2D environments

[Showcase and playable game](https://developers.openai.com/showcase/hollowflux)

Consult when an environmental effect should shape gameplay. Explore how water responds to movement and weapon attacks, carries objects, and conducts electricity while characters remain readable against the effects.

Void Explorer, Sunwake, and Hollowflux are visual and build-process references here; no public source repositories were found. Evergrow's source is public, but no repository-wide open-source license was found.
